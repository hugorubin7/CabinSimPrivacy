CabinSim — Account Deletion Request

You can request the deletion of your CabinSim account and all associated data by sending an email to:

📧 hugorubin7@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Please include the email address associated with your CabinSim account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, password)
  • Your profile information (name, airline)
  • Your usage statistics (scenarios completed, scores, badges)
  • All data stored under your user ID in our database

Retention period

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request. No data is retained after deletion.
